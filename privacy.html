潮汐釣魚村 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 12 日

潮汐釣魚村是一款可以跟大家一起釣魚、聊天的遊戲。我們只收集讓遊戲能玩所需要的最少資料，不賣資料、沒有廣告、沒有廣告追蹤。

我們收集什麼

  • 匿名帳號編號（打開遊戲時自動建立，不是你的真名）
  • 你在遊戲裡取的名字
  • 遊戲進度：金幣、釣到的魚、圖鑑、衣服、家具、小島、寵物、好友名單等
  • 你在廣場留言板寫的留言；被別人檢舉的聊天內容（只有被檢舉時才會存下來）
  • email（只有你自己選擇綁定時才有，用來換手機時登回同一個角色）
  • 推播通知用的裝置代碼（只有你自己打開推播時才有）
  • 遊戲出錯時的錯誤紀錄（不含個人資料）

我們不收集：真實姓名、電話、地址、所在位置、通訊錄、照片。

用在哪裡

  • 讓遊戲能玩、保存你的進度
  • 多人連線（讓大家看得到彼此、一起聊天）
  • 防止作弊
  • 處理檢舉，維護大家的安全

聊天

一般聊天是即時傳給附近或同一個房間的玩家，伺服器不保存聊天紀錄。只有聊天被檢舉時，那則內容會存下來給管理員處理，最多保存 30 天就自動刪除。

資料存在哪裡

  • Supabase：雲端資料庫，存帳號和遊戲進度
  • GitHub Pages：放遊戲網頁本身

保存多久

  • 遊戲進度：你的帳號存在期間都會保存
  • 被檢舉的聊天內容：30 天
  • 刪除帳號後：你的遊戲資料和登入帳號會立刻刪除，備份最晚在 30 天內清除

兒童

第一次進遊戲時會詢問出生年份。未滿 13 歲的玩家會自動進入兒童安全模式：只能用固定的快速聊天句子、看不到別人自由打字的內容、不能在留言板留言，也不會收集 email。

怎麼刪除我的資料

請看 刪除帳號說明。在遊戲的「設定」最下面就能自己刪除。

Tide Fishing Village — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Tide Fishing Village is a game where you fish and chat with other players. We collect only what the game needs to work. We do not sell data, show ads, or use ad tracking.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account ID (created automatically; not your real name)
  • The name you choose in the game
  • Game progress: coins, fish, collection book, clothes, furniture, island, pets, friends list
  • Posts you write on the plaza message board; chat messages only when someone reports them
  • Email address, only if you choose to link one so you can keep your progress on another phone
  • A push-notification device token, only if you turn notifications on
  • Error logs (no personal information)

We do not collect your real name, phone number, address, location, contacts or photos.

How we use it

  • To run the game and save your progress
  • Multiplayer (seeing and chatting with other players)
  • Preventing cheating
  • Handling reports to keep players safe

Chat

Chat is delivered live to nearby players and is not stored on our servers. A message is stored only if it is reported, so a moderator can review it, and it is deleted automatically after 30 days.

Where data is stored

  • Supabase (cloud database: accounts and game progress)
  • GitHub Pages (hosts the game website)

How long we keep it

  • Game progress: as long as your account exists
  • Reported chat messages: 30 days
  • After you delete your account: game data and login are deleted immediately; backups are cleared within 30 days

Children

The game asks for the player's birth year on first launch. Players under 13 are placed in a child-safe mode: they can use only preset quick-chat phrases, cannot see other players' free-typed messages, cannot post on the message board, and no email is collected.

Deleting your data

See How to delete your account. You can delete your account yourself at the bottom of Settings in the game.
